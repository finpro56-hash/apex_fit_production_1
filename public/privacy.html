Privacy

Apex Fit stores fitness and nutrition information in your private Firebase account. We use Firebase Authentication for sign-in and Cloud Firestore for your application data.

AI processing

When you use AI food analysis or Apex Coach, the information required for that request is sent to the configured Gemini API provider for processing. Food photos are sent for analysis only and are not intentionally stored by Apex Fit on its server.

Data access

Your Firestore data is protected by rules that require your authenticated Firebase user ID. Server AI endpoints also require a valid Firebase ID token.

Deletion

You can request deletion of your Apex Fit account data from the application owner. Firebase authentication and Firestore data should be deleted together when an account is removed.

Important

Apex Fit is a fitness and nutrition tracking tool, not a healthcare provider. AI-generated nutrition and fitness information is approximate and should not be treated as medical advice.
