Health & AI Safety

Apex Fit is designed for adults 18+. Its AI features provide general fitness and nutrition information, not medical advice.

AI nutrition estimates

Calories and macros from photos, text, or AI estimates can be wrong because portions, ingredients, preparation, and image quality are uncertain. Review results before logging or relying on them.

Workout safety

Use appropriate technique and loads for your experience. Do not use AI output to push through significant pain, injury, dizziness, chest symptoms, or breathing difficulty.

Urgent situations

If you have severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, contact your local emergency service immediately. Apex Coach cannot diagnose or manage emergencies.

Higher-risk situations

For pregnancy, eating disorders, serious medical conditions, medication questions, significant injuries, or medically prescribed diets, consult a qualified healthcare professional before changing your exercise or nutrition plan.
